    CS / 01

    Sole UX designer · Rural lead generation

    “Sell more pumps” became “capture a lead in 20 seconds.”

    Field research in Nepal showed that the users were being asked to do too much at once. I designed a redefined work process, an interface that reflected the change, and built online and offline tools that empowered them.

    27+ → 4 fields · 6+ min → ~20 sec

    • Workflow Design

    CS / 02

    Solo UX designer · Construction platform

    Design that keeps up with a product that never slows down.

    What happens when the product scales without the principal foundations? I built a design system, structured handover documentations, and changed the sprint structure so that the existing limitations wouldn’t hinder the company’s pace.

    ~70% fewer handoff meetings

    • Design Systems
    • Pipeline Improvement

    CS / 03

    Lead product designer · Gov. IoT platform

    From pump dashboard to project platform.

    A request to monitor solar pumps for a government agency became a lightweight platform connecting thousands of project records to live pump data, by negotiating what the first version should include and what could wait.

    More about me

    A constant work in progress

    My journey in design has been anything but linear, but one belief never changed: design is a way to solve problems with ingenuity and creativity.

    1. Late 2000s

      The mustache era

      Discovered I could add mustaches to people in Photoshop. Phlearn tutorials did the rest.

    2. Around then

      Book covers

      My watercolors led to book cover gigs. Preparing them for print led me to graphic design, then logos.

    3. 2016

      Going full-time

      Learned the craft on the job, then worked across industries as a graphic designer, logo designer, branding specialist and brand consultant.

    4. 2018

      Falling for UX

      Curiosity led me to Human-Computer Interaction and a new way to design, bringing together problem-solving, research and an understanding of people. I went from brand consultant to UX designer.

    5. 2024

      HCI in Germany

      A master’s to deepen my research skills. A new country meant unlearning and relearning.

    6. Now (current)

      UX Designer

      Also a hobbyist digital artist and occasional visual consultant.

      Painting • Illustration • Photography • 3D • Front-end • Branding

    Next: new challenges, and real-world problems to solve through thoughtful, human-centered design.
